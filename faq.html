FAQ

Good to know

Can we arrive at the arboretum early?

Please do. The gardens are open to the public — take a stroll, find the Rose Garden, and settle in. The ceremony begins promptly at 5:00 pm, so please be in your seat by then.

Are children invited?

Regrettably, we're only able to include children whose names appear on your invitation.

How do we get to the after party?

A shuttle will run from the arboretum to Watts & Ward downtown once the celebration wraps up. Pickup times and details are coming — we'll post them here.

Where do we park at the arboretum?

Ample street parking along Beryl Rd in front of the Arboretum.
