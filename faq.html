FAQ

Good to know

Can we arrive at the arboretum early?

Please do. The gardens open to our guests at 4:30 pm — take a stroll, find the Rose Garden, and settle in. The ceremony begins promptly at 5:00 pm, so please be in your seat by then.

Are children invited?

We love your little ones, but we're only able to include children whose names appear on your invitation.

If your children are joining us and get fussy during the ceremony, we'd be grateful if you'd step out of the Rose Garden with them until they've settled — the surrounding gardens are a lovely place to wander.

How do we get to the after party?

A shuttle will run from the arboretum to Watts & Ward downtown once the celebration wraps up. Pickup times and details are coming — we'll post them here.

Where do we park at the arboretum?

Free parking is available in the arboretum's lot at 4415 Beryl Rd. More parking details to come.
