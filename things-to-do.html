Things to Do

Eat, drink, and explore Raleigh

Raleigh is the City of Oaks — plenty to see and eat between events. A few of our favorites below. Reservations are recommended for the special-occasion spots, especially on a wedding weekend.

See & Do
Art & Culture

North Carolina Museum of Art

World-class collection plus a 164-acre outdoor museum park with trails and sculpture. Free general admission.

2110 Blue Ridge Rd

Art & Culture

NC Museum of Natural Sciences

The largest natural history museum in the Southeast, right downtown. Also free — look for the giant globe.

11 W Jones St

Outdoors

Dorothea Dix Park

300+ acres of rolling fields with the best skyline views in the city. Great for a morning walk or picnic.

1030 Richardson Dr

Outdoors

Pullen Park

A classic — paddle boats, a 1911 carousel, and a miniature train. One of the oldest amusement parks in the country.

520 Ashe Ave

Local Flavor

State Farmers Market

Open daily. NC produce, flowers, and a legendary biscuit breakfast at the market restaurants.

1201 Agriculture St

Local Flavor

Videri Chocolate Factory

Bean-to-bar chocolate factory in the Warehouse District — free self-guided tours and very good samples.

327 W Davie St

Fun & Games

Boxcar Bar + Arcade

Retro arcade games, pinball, and local beer on tap. A short walk from the after party venue.

330 W Davie St

Sneak Peek

JC Raulston Arboretum

Yes — our wedding venue is a public garden. Stroll the 10 acres of themed gardens before the big day. Free to visit.

4415 Beryl Rd

Eat · Special Occasion

Death & Taxes

Live-fire cooking in a moody century-old former bank. Order the grilled oysters and the burnt honey pie.

105 W Hargett St

Second Empire

Refined Southern dining in a historic 1879 Victorian house. Raleigh's classic celebration restaurant.

330 Hillsborough St

Crawford & Son

Chef-driven neighborhood spot in Person Street — seasonal, precise, and worth the reservation.

618 N Person St

Eat · Crowd Pleasers

Brewery Bhavana

Dim sum, craft beer, a bookstore, and a flower shop under one roof. It shouldn't work, but it's one of the best rooms in the city.

218 S Blount St

Bida Manda

Beloved Laotian restaurant next door to Bhavana. Get the crispy pork belly soup — trust us.

222 S Blount St

Poole's Diner

Ashley Christensen's original — the macaroni au gratin is legendary. Great for brunch too.

426 S McDowell St

St. Roch Fine Oysters

NC seafood by way of New Orleans. Oysters, Creole flavors, and a lively bar.

223 S Wilmington St

Sam Jones BBQ

Whole-hog eastern NC barbecue from one of the state's great pitmaster families. Casual and essential.

502 W Lenoir St

Taverna Agora

Greek taverna with one of the best rooftop patios downtown.

326 Hillsborough St

Drink

Watts & Ward

Underground speakeasy-style cocktail lounge — and conveniently, our after party venue. Consider this a preview.

200 S Blount St

The Crunkleton

Serious classic cocktails and an encyclopedic whiskey wall.

216 S Wilmington St

Whiskey Kitchen

Warehouse District bar and kitchen — relaxed, with a deep brown-liquor list and a good patio.

201 W Martin St
