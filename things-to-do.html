Things to Do

Make a weekend of it

Raleigh is the City of Oaks — plenty to see between events. A few of our favorites:

Art & Culture

North Carolina Museum of Art

World-class collection plus a 164-acre outdoor museum park with trails and sculpture. Free general admission.

2110 Blue Ridge Rd

Art & Culture

NC Museum of Natural Sciences

The largest natural history museum in the Southeast, right downtown. Also free — look for the giant globe.

11 W Jones St

Outdoors

Dorothea Dix Park

300+ acres of rolling fields with the best skyline views in the city. Great for a morning walk or picnic.

1030 Richardson Dr

Outdoors

Pullen Park

A classic — paddle boats, a 1911 carousel, and a miniature train. One of the oldest amusement parks in the country.

520 Ashe Ave

Local Flavor

State Farmers Market

Open daily. NC produce, flowers, and a legendary biscuit breakfast at the market restaurants.

1201 Agriculture St

Local Flavor

Videri Chocolate Factory

Bean-to-bar chocolate factory in the Warehouse District — free self-guided tours and very good samples.

327 W Davie St

Fun & Games

Boxcar Bar + Arcade

Retro arcade games, pinball, and local beer on tap. A short walk from the after party venue.

330 W Davie St

Sneak Peek

JC Raulston Arboretum

Yes — our wedding venue is a public garden. Stroll the 10 acres of themed gardens before the big day. Free to visit.

4415 Beryl Rd
