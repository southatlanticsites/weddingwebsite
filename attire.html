Attire Inspiration

Garden Semi-formal

Please dress semi-formally for an outdoor celebration on grass. Light, breathable fabrics are recommended — linen, cotton, and natural tones work well in the garden setting.

For the guys

Dress shirts, vests, and sport coats are welcome. Ties optional.

For the gals

Cocktail dresses, midi or maxi lengths in elevated but comfortable fabrics. Please avoid heels — wedges, flats, or block heels are better suited for the lawn.
