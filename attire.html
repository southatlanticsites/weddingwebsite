Attire Inspiration

Garden formal

The ceremony and dinner are outdoors in the arboretum gardens, so think elegant but garden-friendly.

The Vibe

Dress for the gardens

Cocktail dresses, midi and maxi dresses, suits and sport coats (tie optional). September evenings in Raleigh are warm — light fabrics are your friend.

Practical Notes

Watch your step

We'll be on garden paths and lawn for part of the evening — block heels, wedges, or flats will treat you better than stilettos. The after party is a cozy underground lounge, so you'll be comfortable straight through.
